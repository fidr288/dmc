Urgent: Phone Scam Warning / 紧急安全通知：防范电话诈骗

Individuals pretending to represent Dominion Medical Centre are calling patients claiming they are owed a cash reward or refund, and asking them to buy Apple gift cards or credit vouchers.

This is a scam. We will never:

  • Offer cash rewards or unexpected financial bonuses over the phone.
  • Ask you to purchase gift cards, vouchers, or cryptocurrency.
  • Request banking passwords, PINs, or credit card details via phone or text.

What to do: Hang up immediately. Call us directly on 09-6204986 to verify any call. If you have lost money, contact NZ Police and report it to netsafe.org.nz.

有诈骗分子冒充本诊所致电病患，声称有现金奖励或退款，并指示购买Apple礼品卡或点数券。

这是一项诈骗行为。本诊所绝不会：

  • 透过电话提供现金奖励或财务花红。
  • 要求您购买礼品卡、点数券或加密货币。
  • 透过电话或简讯索取银行密码、PIN码或信用卡资讯。

您应该怎么做：请立即挂断电话，并拨打官方电话 09-6204986 核实。如遭受金钱损失，请联系新西兰警方并向 netsafe.org.nz 举报。

Lab Results Policy / 检查报告政策

Due to The Privacy Act we can only release your information to a nominated person with your consent.

Unfortunately we are not able to contact everyone regarding their test results, therefore we do not routinely do so if the results are normal. However, if you have a significant abnormal result, we will always endeavour to contact you. It is important that you keep all contact details updated with reception so we can make contact. If results are of a critical nature, and you haven’t heard back from us, please ring to check with one of the nurses, but be aware that results can take up to a week to come back. Please note that the nurses are not qualified to interpret results in detail so in some circumstances you may need to make an appointment with your doctor to have your results explained.

根据《隐私法》，我们只能在您同意的情况下将您的个人资料透露给指定的人。

我们无法就每个人的检验结果主动告知，如检查结果正常，我们通常不会主动联系您。如果您的检验报告有重大异常，我们将会主动尽力联络您。若您的联系方式有任何变动（如电话和电子邮件），请务必和我们尽快更改，以便及时联系。如检验项目起到关键性的作用，而您还未收到我们的消息，请致电我们的护士以进行核对。请注意特殊检验项目的结果可能会需要数周才会出来。某些情况下，护士可能无法详细解释结果和治疗方法，在此情况下，您将需要预约医生以对检验结果进行讨论。
