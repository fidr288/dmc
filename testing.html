Urgent Security Notice: Phone Scam Warning

Please be aware that a phone scam is currently targeting patients in our community.

We have been notified that individuals pretending to represent our medical centre are contacting patients by phone, claiming they are entitled to a cash reward or refund from the clinic. The scammers then instruct the patient to purchase Apple gift cards or credit vouchers to claim the money.

This is a scam. Dominion Medical Centre will never:

  • Offer cash rewards or unexpected financial bonuses over the phone.
  • Ask you to purchase gift cards, vouchers, or cryptocurrency for any reason.
  • Request your private banking passwords, PINs, or credit card details via phone or text.

What you should do:

  • Hang up immediately if you receive a suspicious call matching this description.
  • Verify directly: If you are ever unsure whether a communication from us is legitimate, hang up and call us back directly using our official clinic phone number (09-6204986).
  • Report it: If you or a family member have lost money to this scam, contact the New Zealand Police immediately and report the incident to Netsafe (netsafe.org.nz).

The safety and privacy of our patients are our highest priorities. Thank you for your vigilance.

紧急安全通知：防范电话诈骗

请注意：目前有一项电话诈骗行为正在针对我们社区的病患。

我们接获通知，有诈骗分子冒充本诊所的代表与病患联系，声称病患有权领取诊所发放的现金奖励或退款。随后，诈骗分子会指示病患购买Apple礼品卡（gift cards）或信用点数券以领取该笔款项。

这是一项诈骗行为。本诊所（Dominion Medical Centre）绝不会：

  • 透过电话提供未经证实的现金奖励或意外的财务花红。
  • 以任何理由要求您购买礼品卡、点数券或加密货币。
  • 透过电话或简讯要求您提供个人银行密码、PIN码或信用卡详细资讯。

您应该怎么做：

  • 请立即挂断电话：如果您接到符合上述说明的可疑电话。
  • 直接核实：如果您不确定来自本诊所的通讯是否属实，请立即挂断电话，并拨打我们诊所的官方电话号码直接与我们联系（09-6204986）。
  • 进行通报：如果您或您的家人因该诈骗行为而遭受金钱损失，请立即联系新西兰警方（New Zealand Police），并向Netsafe（netsafe.org.nz）进行举报。

病患的安全与隐私是我们最重视的首要任务。感谢您的警惕与配合。
