Urgent: Phone Scam Warning 紧急安全通知：防范电话诈骗

Individuals pretending to represent Dominion Medical Centre are calling patients claiming they are owed a cash reward or refund, and asking them to buy Apple gift cards or credit vouchers.

This is a scam. We will never:

  • Offer cash rewards or unexpected financial bonuses over the phone.
  • Ask you to purchase gift cards, vouchers, or cryptocurrency.
  • Request banking passwords, PINs, or credit card details via phone or text.

What to do: Hang up immediately. Call us directly on 09-6204986 to verify any call. If you have lost money, contact NZ Police and report it to netsafe.org.nz.

有诈骗分子冒充本诊所致电病患，声称有现金奖励或退款，并指示购买Apple礼品卡或点数券。

这是一项诈骗行为。本诊所绝不会：

  • 透过电话提供现金奖励或财务花红。
  • 要求您购买礼品卡、点数券或加密货币。
  • 透过电话或简讯索取银行密码、PIN码或信用卡资讯。

您应该怎么做：请立即挂断电话，并拨打官方电话 09-6204986 核实。如遭受金钱损失，请联系新西兰警方并向 netsafe.org.nz 举报。
